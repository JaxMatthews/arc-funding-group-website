Our History

Two decades of placing capital nationwide

2003

ARC Funding Group, LLC is established

Founded as the successor to Lake Creek Financial, a nationwide bridge lending operation.

2003 – 2008

Bridge lending and the launch of loan brokerage

ARC provided bridge loans directly while building out commercial loan brokerage services alongside its own lending activity.

2008 – Present

A dedicated focus on loan origination

ARC transitioned away from lending its own capital to focus exclusively on placing commercial loans with the lenders best positioned to fund them, across permanent loans, construction financing, structured deals, and bridge loans nationwide.

What Sets Us Apart

A different kind of loan placement

  • We do the legwork We save borrowers time, effort, and money by evaluating and preparing each package before it goes to our affiliate network of lenders.
  • Hundreds of lenders, not one referral Borrowers gain access to literally hundreds of potential lenders instead of a single point of contact and a single shot at approval.
  • We stay with you the whole way We remain directly involved throughout the entire loan process, rather than handing clients off midway to someone new.
  • We keep building better tools We continuously develop new and improved financing vehicles to keep pace with how commercial lending actually works today.
